## Known scientific limitations

- The MVP does not bundle or process a clipped Wadi Hauran GeoTIFF or the 3.6 GB Gale raster. Topography, slope, roughness, and thermal comparison remain explicitly unavailable/partial.
- The default Wadi Hauran ↔ Gale Crater view intentionally has no numeric PASI until paired measurements are loaded. Uploaded values are not accepted without a source ID and processing method.
- Planetary reference coordinates are shown as an atlas reference layer and are explicitly labeled as not Earth-projected.
- Field access, security, safety, and boundary validation for Wadi Hauran require independent local verification.
